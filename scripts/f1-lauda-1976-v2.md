**The loop reads:** "...That's why... this man just walked away from the world championship. On purpose."

## Why it holds

- **Scroll-stop:** the first sentence is a paradox (who walks away from a championship?), and "On purpose." lands the twist inside 3 seconds.
- **Open loop at 10 s:** "To understand why, rewind eighty-four days" promises an answer and doesn't pay it off until the last 15 seconds.
- **Pattern interrupts:** the rewind and fast-forward are game-style effects that reset attention at the two turning points.
- **Echo:** "Lap two" happens twice, the crash and the decision.
- **Payoff then loop:** the meaning lands, then "That's why..." hands straight back to the opening line, so viewers rewatch without noticing.

## Fact notes

- Nürburgring crash: 1 August 1976, lap 2. Fuji (Japanese GP): 24 October 1976. 1 Aug → 24 Oct = 84 days.
- Trapped in the burning car until other drivers pulled him out. Doctors feared for his life.
- Back racing at Monza 42 days after the crash (Lauda's own count), finished 4th with wounds still bleeding.
- Fuji: led Hunt by 3 points; withdrew in heavy rain within the first two laps (most accounts: lap 2); Hunt won the title by 1 point (69–68).
- "His life was worth more than a title" is how his reason is widely reported; voiced as "he said".
