# He Raced F1 Before He Could Drive? 🤯

Broad-audience short, exactly 60 s, continuous voiceover, looped ending (the last line runs straight into the first).

## Script v1 (awaiting approval before voiceover)

Story shape: impossible-sounding question -> the kid on the grid -> the contradiction (no licence) -> doubters ->
he proves them right to worry and wrong to doubt -> twist (the rule book already changed behind him) -> payoff
(first win, four titles) -> back to the question. Transitions carried by the idea, no "then"/"next".

1. How do you race in Formula One when you're too young to drive a car?
2. Melbourne, 2015. Cars that top three hundred kilometres an hour roar onto the grid.
3. In one of them sits a seventeen-year-old.
4. Max Verstappen: the youngest driver in Formula One history.
5. Back home, he isn't even old enough for a driving licence. In Belgium, you have to be eighteen.
6. The paddock is furious: too young, too wild, someone will get hurt.
7. Two weeks later in Malaysia, he finishes seventh and scores points. Nobody that young had ever done it; the record holder was nineteen.
8. But the rule makers had already made up their minds.
9. Months before his debut, they had quietly rewritten the rule book.
10. From 2016, every driver must be eighteen, and hold a real road licence.
11. The door he walked through slammed shut behind him.
12. Did it slow him down? Not for a second.
13. At eighteen, he won his first Grand Prix, the youngest winner ever.
14. He went on to win four world titles in a row.
15. And it all began with one question nobody could answer...

(loops to line 1: "...one question nobody could answer... How do you race in Formula One when you're too young to drive a car?")

## Facts and sources

- Debut: 2015 Australian GP (15 March 2015), aged 17 years 166 days, the youngest F1 starter ever.
  Only 15 cars started that race, so the script gives no car count.
- Road licence: in January 2015 he had passed the road-car theory test; he said "In Belgium you need to be 18 for that,
  so I'm not there yet." ([grandprix.com, 12 Jan 2015](https://www.grandprix.com/ns/ns29751.html))
- First points: 7th at the 2015 Malaysian GP (29 March, two weeks after Melbourne), aged 17 years 180 days; he beat
  Daniil Kvyat's record (19 years 324 days) by more than two years.
  ([Guinness World Records](https://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/youngest-driver-to-claim-a-formula-one-world-championship-point),
  [GrandPrix247](https://www.grandprix247.com/formula-1-news/verstappen-adds-another-record-with-first-f1-points))
- Rule change: announced by the FIA in December 2014 (before his debut), applied from 2016: drivers must be 18,
  hold a valid road driving licence and pass a test on the Sporting Regulations. His signing prompted it.
  ([PlanetF1](https://www.planetf1.com/news/fia-sets-minimum-age-of-18), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/2014/12/03/minimum-age-for-f1-drivers-to-be-introduced))
- Caveat: the FIA has since added a discretionary exception for exceptional 17-year-olds, so the script says the door
  was "slammed shut" in 2016 and does not claim the record can never be broken.
- First win: 2016 Spanish GP aged 18, the youngest race winner ever. Titles: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 (four in a row);
  the script says "four in a row", which stays true whatever happens after 2024.
- Not verified, so left out: when exactly he passed his road driving test.
