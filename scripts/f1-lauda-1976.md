## Why it holds attention

- **Hook (0–3 s):** visual + text + voice all say the same shocking thing at once. Nobody expects "last rites" in an F1 video.
- **Open loop (7 s):** "not the bravest thing he did" keeps the question open until the last 10 seconds.
- **Paid-off promise:** the hook promises "42 days later" and Monza delivers it. The opening question is answered by the walk-away.
- **Echo:** "Lap two. Again." links the crash to the decision, so the ending lands harder.
- **Change of scene every 3–6 s**, and the on-screen text tells the whole story with the sound off.
- **Loop:** the last helmet shot matches the first, so replays run straight on.

## Fact notes (checked)

- Crash: Nürburgring, 1 August 1976, lap 2. Lauda was the reigning 1975 champion. A priest gave him his last rites in hospital.
- Return: Italian GP at Monza, 12 September 1976. Finished 4th, wounds still bleeding. "42 days" is Lauda's own figure; some sources say 40 or 41.
- Japan (Fuji): Lauda led Hunt by 3 points. He withdrew in heavy rain within the first two laps (most accounts: on lap 2). Hunt finished 3rd and won the title by 1 point.
- Final points: Hunt 69, Lauda 68. Shown as 68 vs 65 going into Fuji.
- The "life worth more than a title" line is how his reason is widely reported. It's on screen as a quote; voiced as "he said".
- Visuals: keep the cars free of sponsor and team logos (Ferrari, McLaren, Marlboro). The low-poly Lauda should be clearly stylised, not a photoreal likeness. Show the fire as abstract shapes, never injuries.
