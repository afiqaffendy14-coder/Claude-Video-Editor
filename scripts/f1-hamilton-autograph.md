## 1. Video concept

- **Title:** A 10-Year-Old Made a Promise to an F1 Boss. He Kept It.
- **Core story:** at the Autosport Awards in 1995, 10-year-old Lewis Hamilton told McLaren boss Ron Dennis he wanted
  to race his cars. Dennis wrote "phone me in nine years" in the boy's autograph book, which Lewis's dad had made.
  His dad worked several jobs to pay for karting. Three years later, McLaren signed Lewis at 13. He made his F1 debut
  with McLaren in 2007, finished third in his first race and became world champion a year later.
- **Target audience:** anyone. It's a story about a parent, a child and a promise. F1 is just the setting.
- **Main curiosity angle:** "Did the boy ever make that phone call?"

>

## 3. Retention analysis (prediction, not data)

| Section | Potential problem | Why viewers might leave |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0–5 s) | Strong. A child's nerve is a surprise. | Low risk. |
| Backstory (5–25 s) | **Twenty seconds of background before the answer** | The hook makes you ask "what did the boss say?" Then the script goes back to the estate, the dad, the awards night and who Dennis was. The answer is held back too long. |
| "Nine years… kept racing" (~30–35 s) | Filler | It tells us nothing new and it's predictable. |
| Twist and debut (35–55 s) | Good | "Didn't wait nine years" is a real reversal. |
| Ending | Good, but plain | It doesn't call back to the dad or the book. |

**Biggest retention risk:** 0:05–0:25. The hook asks a question and the script answers it 20 seconds later, after a
stack of background.

**Fix:** answer straight away ("phone me in nine years"). That reply opens a new question: will he ever make that
call? Then the background is no longer a delay. It becomes the stakes, because his family had to keep paying for
nine years. Cut the filler. End on the homemade autograph book, which links the dad and the promise.

## Fact sheet (for the description)

- 1995 Autosport Awards: Hamilton, aged 10, was there as a karting champion. He approached Ron Dennis, who signed his
  autograph book and wrote that he should phone him in nine years (RacingNews365, F1 Chronicle, GPFans).
- His dad had made the autograph book. Lewis went over before his dad had finished explaining who Dennis was
  (RacingNews365). "I don't know what possessed me" is Hamilton's own description.
- The exact wording varies: some sources add "we'll sort something out then". The script uses only the part all
  sources agree on.
- Anthony Hamilton worked several jobs at once (three or four, depending on the source) to fund karting. He grew up
  on a council estate in Stevenage. Redundancy and remortgage stories are **not** supported, so they are left out.
- 1998: McLaren signed him to its young driver programme at 13 (GPFans "20 years ago").
- 2007: F1 debut for McLaren in Australia, finishing third. 2008: world champion with McLaren in Brazil.
- The script avoids "youngest ever champion" (true in 2008, but beaten since) and avoids saying Dennis "phoned"
  (not confirmed).
