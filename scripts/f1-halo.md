# The Part F1 Wanted to Chainsaw Off

Broad-audience short, exactly 60 s, continuous voiceover, looped ending (the last line runs straight into the first).
Same pipeline, look and narrator as `videos/pitstop-hospital` (ElevenLabs "F1 Narrator", blocky voxel style).

## Script v2: "car on your head" hook (current, awaiting approval before voiceover)

Owner picked this hook from four options (others: "holds up a bus", "chainsaw reversal", "everyone hated it").
Opens inside Monza 2021, goes back to 2018 to explain the hate, then the bus test and Grosjean, returns to
Monza for the payoff, and lands on the Wolff twist. 187 words, about 60 s at the narrator's pace.

1. Imagine a Formula One car landing on your head.
2. Lewis Hamilton doesn't have to.
3. At Monza in 2021, Max Verstappen's car landed on top of his.
4. Between his head and the tyre was a curved bar called the halo.
5. When it arrived in 2018, fans called it ugly, and some drivers agreed.
6. Mercedes boss Toto Wolff said, "If you give me a chainsaw, I would take it off."
7. But it's built to hold about twelve tonnes: a double-decker bus.
8. In Bahrain in 2020, Romain Grosjean hit a steel barrier at nearly two hundred kilometres an hour.
9. His car split in two and burst into flames.
10. The halo shielded his head as the car tore through the steel.
11. Nearly half a minute later, he climbed out of the fire.
12. Grosjean had doubted it. Now he called it the greatest thing F1 had brought in.
13. Back at Monza, Verstappen's rear tyre hit Hamilton's helmet, and the halo pushed it off.
14. Hamilton said it saved his neck. His team boss said it had definitely saved his life.
15. That boss was Toto Wolff.
16. So next time you call it ugly, just...

(loops to line 1: "So next time you call it ugly, just... imagine a Formula One car landing on your head.")

## Wording rules

- The narrator never claims on its own authority that the halo "saved lives". Only the people involved say it,
  and each time the script names who said it (Hamilton: "saved my neck"; Wolff: "definitely saved Lewis's life").
- Crashes are shown abstractly, with no injuries (style guide). Grosjean's fire is shown as orange voxel glow, not a person burning.
- Real people are shown as stylised block characters, never photoreal. No team or sponsor logos (Mercedes car stays
  generic silver; Haas generic white/grey; Red Bull generic navy).

## Facts and sources

- Halo mandatory on F1 cars from the 2018 season, forced through by the FIA on safety grounds despite opposition from teams.
- Toto Wolff, Mercedes W09 launch, Silverstone, 22 Feb 2018: "If you give me a chainsaw, I would take it off."
  ([Motorsport.com](https://www.motorsport.com/f1/news/wolff-would-take-a-chainsaw-to-the-halo-1007659/1392755/),
  [The Drive](https://www.thedrive.com/accelerator/18782/mercedes-benz-motorsport-exec-would-take-a-chainsaw-to-a-halo-safety-device))
- Load test: 116 kN vertical (≈11.8 t) at the top of the halo. James Allison (Mercedes) and Matt Morris (McLaren) compared
  it to a double-decker bus. The script says "about twelve tonnes" and uses the bus as a comparison only.
  ([Autosport](https://www.autosport.com/f1/news/134343/thisissearch), [F1.com Tech Tuesday](https://www.formula1.com/en/latest/article/tech-tuesday-a-close-look-at-the-halo-and-how-it-saved-hamiltons-neck-in.68Ajvw12Xza2P3JvuXGRO3))
- Grosjean, Bahrain GP, 29 Nov 2020: hit the barrier at 192 km/h; car split in two and caught fire; out in about 27–28 s.
  He had been sceptical of the halo and afterwards called it the "greatest thing" F1 had brought in.
  ([Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/sport/motorsport/formula-one-romain-grosjean-hit-barrier-at-192kph-with-67g-force-in-fireball-crash-1.77647856),
  [Eurosport](https://www.eurosport.com/formula-1/bahrain-grand-prix/2020/romain-grosjean-says-halo-protection-saved-his-life-in-horrific-f1-crash_sto8013404/story.shtml))
- Monza, Italian GP, 12 Sep 2021: Verstappen's car landed on Hamilton's; Verstappen's right-rear tyre hit Hamilton's helmet and
  was deflected by the halo. Hamilton: it "saved my neck". Wolff: the halo "definitely saved Lewis's life".
  ([ESPN](https://africa.espn.com/f1/story/_/id/32196837/lewis-hamilton-says-halo-saved-life-surprised-max-verstappen-check-him),
  [Crash.net](https://www.crash.net/f1/news/987486/1/wolff-halo-saved-hamilton-s-life-crash-verstappen))
- Not verified, so left out: the halo's exact weight (7 kg is only on fan sites), "titanium" (not in the sources checked),
  and the impact g-force (sources say 53 g or 67 g).
- Held in reserve (cut for length): Zhou Guanyu, Silverstone 2022, car upside down; tweeted "halo saved me today".
