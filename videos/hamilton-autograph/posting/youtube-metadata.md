## Description

A ten-year-old boy walked up to a Formula One team boss and asked for a job. The boss wrote his answer in the boy's autograph book: phone me in nine years.

⏱️ The facts
• 1995: at a motorsport awards dinner, 10-year-old Lewis Hamilton asked McLaren boss Ron Dennis for a drive one day. Dennis wrote in his autograph book that he should phone him in nine years.
• His family had little money. His go-kart was fifth-hand, refurbished by his dad, Anthony, who worked several jobs at once (sources say three or four) to keep him racing.
• At the kart track he was often the only Black kid. He has said he was bullied and racially abused, and that he hid his tears so his dad wouldn't think he wasn't strong. His dad's advice: always do your talking on the track.
• 1998: at 13, he was signed to McLaren's young driver programme, three years after the note.
• 2008, Brazil, last lap, in the rain: at 23, he passed Timo Glock two corners from the end and won the world championship by one point, racing for Ron Dennis's McLaren.
