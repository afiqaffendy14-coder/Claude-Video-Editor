03Monza 20214.60–6.68s · 2.1s

“At Monza in 2021,”

Camera: From up in the tifosi grandstand, long lens, panning with the cars. Action: The two cars braking side by side toward the chicane, the packed stand opposite behind them. Ends on the instant shot 4 starts.

04Monza 20216.68–9.11s · 2.4s

“Max Verstappen's car landed on top of his. Between”

Camera: Rides alongside Hamilton's car in one continuous move, shakes on the landing. Action: Real time: Verstappen's right wheels hit the yellow sausage kerb, his car flies up and rolls over, lands on Hamilton's halo with a bounce, and both slide into the gravel and slow to rest.
