Frames from the 60 s animation test v2 ·start / middle / end of each shot · movement pass (lighting, colour and effects come later) · orange = open review notes

09Car launch stage, 201817.42–19.12s · 1.7s

“boss Toto Wolff said, "If”

Camera: Front 3/4 of the car, Wolff right behind its cockpit. Action: Wolff talks to the room, a hand opening as he makes his point, a glance down at the halo and back up.

Validator: clean

10Car launch stage, 201819.12–21.86s · 2.7s

“you give me a chainsaw, I would take it off." But”

Camera: Same angle as shot 9. Action: Crouched behind the car, he lifts the chainsaw from its case with its weight, rises, turns with two steps and holds it across his body at the halo, leaning back with a grin.

Validator: right foot slides 8 mm once

11FIA test rig21.86–24.19s · 2.3s

“it's built to hold about twelve tonnes:”

Camera: Low, looking up into the press. Action: The ram comes down onto the halo; the display on the beam climbs 0t → 3t → 12t.

12Hangar24.19–25.58s · 1.4s

“a double-decker bus. In”

Camera: Low by the stand, looking up, tilting down with the bus. Action: The crane lowers the red double-decker bus onto the halo; a small rebound, and it holds.

13Bahrain 2020, night25.58–27.36s · 1.8s

“Bahrain in 2020, Romain”

Camera: Down the track under the floodlights. Action: Lap 1: the pack under the floodlights, Grosjean's white car among them.

14Bahrain 202027.36–31.48s · 4.1s

“Grosjean hit a steel barrier at nearly 200 kilometres an hour.”

Camera: Chase camera along the left edge, braking after the hit. Action: Flat out with the pack, the white car turns sharply right across the track and into the steel barrier; it stays in frame throughout.

15Bahrain 202031.48–34.44s · 3.0s

“His car split in two and burst into flames.”

Camera: From above. Action: The car tears in two: the front half goes through the barrier, the back half spins away and catches fire.

16Bahrain 202034.44–37.83s · 3.4s

“The halo shielded his head as the car tore through the steel.”

Camera: Close on the front half. Action: The front half sits in the barrier; the top rail rides up over the halo instead of into the helmet.
