## Description

In 2018, Mercedes boss Toto Wolff said he'd take a chainsaw to it. Three years later, he said it had definitely saved Lewis Hamilton's life.

The halo: a curved bar around the driver's head that F1 made compulsory, and almost nobody wanted. Then it held firm as Romain Grosjean's car tore through a steel barrier in Bahrain, and it pushed Max Verstappen's tyre off Hamilton's helmet at Monza.

⏱️ The facts
• 2018: the halo becomes mandatory in F1.
• Toto Wolff, Feb 2018: "If you give me a chainsaw, I would take it off."
• Load test: about 12 tonnes, roughly the weight of a double-decker bus.
• Bahrain 2020: Grosjean hits the barrier at 192 km/h; the car splits in two and catches fire. He's out in under 30 seconds and later calls the halo the greatest thing F1 has brought in.
• Monza 2021: Verstappen's car lands on Hamilton's. Hamilton says the halo saved his neck; Wolff says it "definitely saved Lewis's life".
