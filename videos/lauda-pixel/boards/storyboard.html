Lauda: the full short, pixel style (57.45s, 1080×1920)

Ten scenes, each cut just before its line · captions highlight the word being spoken · on-screen text otherwise limited to the DAY counter, P4 and the score boxes · two real frames per scene

01: The hook
0.0 to 3.2s

“Doctors didn't think this man would survive.”

Hospital at night. The scorched red helmet on the bedside table, the patient bandaged in bed, rain on the window. The camera drifts left.

Sound: 8-bit heart-monitor beeps, soft rain behind glass, chiptune bed fades in

02: 42 days later
3.2 to 6.9s

“Forty-two days later... he was racing again.”

Days flicker past the window as a DAY counter runs 0 → 42 (it turns red at 42). Cut to the sunny Monza grid: his red #1 shakes on its idle.

Sound: coin blip per counter tick, power-up jingle on 42, chiptune engine idle

03: The open loop
6.9 to 9.7s

“But that's not the bravest thing he did.”

He launches off screen. The empty grid holds and the colour drains away to dark.

Sound: engine launch and fade, the music drops out

04: The Green Hell
9.7 to 15.4s

“Niki Lauda. World champion. At the track drivers call the Green Hell.”

The Nürburgring from above: dark forested hills and fog, his tiny red car snaking along the track.

Sound: distant engine echo through the hills, low drone

05: Lap two
15.4 to 21.7s

“Lap two. His car hits the barrier and erupts in flames. Drivers drag him out.”

Side view at the barrier. The car wobbles, hits the armco, flames rise (the screen shakes). Other cars stop; three drivers run to him. Shown as fire, never injuries.

Sound: skid, 8-bit explosion on impact, fire crackle, footsteps

06: Fighting
21.7 to 27.3s

“Burned. Toxic fumes in his lungs. For days, he fights for his life.”

Close on the heart monitor: a fast, irregular trace and a red alarm light, steadying to a calm rhythm and a green light on "fights for his life".

Sound: fast irregular monitor beeps that settle into a steady beat

07: Monza
27.3 to 33.4s

“Then, at Monza, he pulls a helmet over still-bleeding wounds... and finishes fourth.”

Close-up: a small fresh stain on the balaclava as his gloves pull the helmet down over it. Cut to the Monza straight: he crosses the line and the pit board flips to P4.

Sound: padded helmet thud, engine pass, small 3-note fanfare on P4

08: Japan
33.4 to 40.3s

“Final race. Japan. He leads James Hunt by three points. And the rain is brutal.”

The camera tilts down from the storm and Mount Fuji to the circuit. Hunt's white car blasts past; score boxes LAUDA 68 / HUNT 65 pop in. The rain doubles on "brutal".

Sound: rain (kept soft), white car pass-by, menu blip on the score boxes

09: The decision
40.3 to 48.3s

“Lap two. Again. He pulls into the pits... and walks away. Hunt wins the title. By one point.”

His car turns into the pits and stops by the mechanic. He climbs out and walks away with his helmet. Hunt's car passes again; the boxes flip to HUNT 69 / LAUDA 68.

Sound: downshift and brakes, engine cut, splashing steps, score-flip blip, a falling 3-note phrase on "By one point"

10: Knowing when to get out
48.3 to 57.5s

“His life, he said, was worth more than a title. The bravest thing isn't getting back in. It's knowing when to get out.”

He walks out past the red pit light; only his car is left. The final shot is his helmet on the wet pit wall, framed exactly like the first shot so the video loops. "FOLLOW FOR MORE F1 STORIES" for the last second.

Sound: rain, the music resolving softly, last monitor-style beep echo
